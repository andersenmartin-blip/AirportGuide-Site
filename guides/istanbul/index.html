Verify your airport code (IST vs. SAW) before booking any transfer. Istanbul Airport (IST) sits far northwest on the European side (Turkish Airlines & most full-service international flights). Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) sits ~40 km southeast on the Asian side (Pegasus, AJet and budget routes). Travelling between IST and SAW takes ~2 hours — allow a 4–6 hour buffer on separate tickets.

1. Know your Istanbul airport & layout

Both airports serve central Istanbul via different metro lines, shuttle buses and Bosphorus bridges/tunnels — your transport plan starts with confirming IST versus SAW.

IST · European Side

Istanbul Airport (Main Terminal)

One vast international/domestic terminal. Walking from outer gates to passport control can take 20–30 minutes — budget extra time before catching a Metro or bus.

SAW · Asian Side

Sabiha Gökçen Terminal

Compact single-terminal layout with the M4 Metro entrance right outside Arrivals. Convenient for Kadıköy and Asian-side districts; crosses via Marmaray or Bosphorus bridges for European-side hotels.

IST ↔ SAW Transfer

No Fast Link Between Airports

HVİST-13 buses or the 4-leg Metro + Marmaray rail chain take ~2 hours. Never book tight self-transfer connections between IST and SAW.

2. Getting from IST or SAW into Istanbul

Rail avoids heavy Istanbul bridge and highway congestion, while 24/7 Havaist coaches and private pickups handle large luggage, steep Sultanahmet/Taksim hills and overnight landings after metro closing time.

Tips for Metro & Istanbulkart

Buy and top up a physical Istanbulkart at the yellow/blue Biletmatik machines right at the IST (M11) or SAW (M4) metro concourses before tapping through the turnstiles. Keep small Turkish lira cash or a contactless bank card handy when machines get busy after simultaneous long-haul arrivals.

Tips for Old City (Sultanahmet) & Beyoğlu

Some pedestrianized Sultanahmet streets close to general cars during daytime tram hours or market periods — confirm your hotel’s nearest drop-off street, and carry offline Google Maps plus a screenshot of the hotel address in Turkish.

3. Turkey mobile data & eSIM on arrival

Remember that Turkey sits outside standard EU/UK “Roam Like at Home” zones — roaming on a home SIM frequently triggers steep daily passes or per-megabyte charges. Having live mobile data immediately helps you check BiTaksi/Uber maps, WhatsApp your hotel and verify transit routes.

Verify your home carrier’s Turkey roaming rate before departing. Note also that Turkish telecom authorities strictly enforce IMEI registration after extended stays (not an issue for short holidays, though installing your eSIM while still on home Wi-Fi guarantees everything activates cleanly on touchdown).

4. Quick Istanbul arrival checklist

  1. Verify IST vs. SAW and check arrival hour. Allow extra walking time inside IST’s huge terminal and remember Metro trains close around midnight.
  2. Save your hotel name, district & phone offline. Distinguish Sultanahmet (Fatih), Taksim/Galata (Beyoğlu) and Kadıköy cleanly.
  3. Sort out Turkey data or eSIM before boarding. Don’t get caught by non-EU daily roaming fees when stepping out of Arrivals.
  4. Carry a bank card & small Turkish lira for Istanbulkart machines. Havaist counters accept cards; some local kiosks prefer cash or card tap.

Common questions

Is Istanbul Airport (IST) close to the city center?

No — IST is ~35–40 km northwest of Taksim/Sultanahmet. The M11 Metro reaches Gayrettepe in ~30 minutes (connecting onto M2), while road drives take 45–90+ minutes depending on traffic.

Does the Metro go from Istanbul Airport to Sultanahmet directly?

You will transfer at least once (typically M11 → Gayrettepe → M2 to Vezneciler/Yenikapı, then walk or take T1 tram/Marmaray) — allow ~60–75 minutes total. With heavy suitcases, Havaist to Aksaray or a direct vehicle transfer avoids multiple station escalators.

What if I land at Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) and stay on the European side?

SAW’s M4 Metro connects directly to Ayrılık Çeşmesi, where the Marmaray rail tunnel crosses under the Bosphorus straight to Sirkeci and Yenikapı in ~60–70 minutes total without bridge traffic.

Are Turkish eSIM plans safe from the Turkey IMEI block?

Yes — tourist travel eSIMs operate via roaming agreements for short stays without requiring local passport-office IMEI tax registration, which applies mainly to physical Turkish SIMs kept active past 120 days.
