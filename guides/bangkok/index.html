1. Main Terminal, SAT-1 & floor levels at BKK

Suvarnabhumi is one huge terminal complex with an attached satellite building (SAT-1). Follow signage vertically — international arrivals, taxis and trains are stacked on different floors.

Main Terminal

Concourses A–G

Houses Immigration, baggage reclaim, customs, hotel meet-and-greet points and every street/rail transport exit into Bangkok.

SAT-1 · APM Shuttle

Satellite 1 Concourse

No direct rail or street exit at SAT-1. If your aircraft docks at an S-gate, take the free underground Automated People Mover (APM) (~2–3 min) to the Main Terminal for passport control and luggage.

Floor Levels

L2 Arrivals · L1 Taxis · B Train

Exit Customs on Level 2. Head down one floor to Level 1 (Gates 4–7) for metered public taxis, Grab pickup and buses, or go down to Basement B for the Airport Rail Link.

2. Getting from BKK into Bangkok

Bangkok expressway traffic can slow road rides significantly at peak hours and during heavy rain, while late-night landings fall after the Airport Rail Link’s midnight cutoff.

Tips for the Airport Rail Link

Follow signs down to Basement B (where currency booths often offer better exchange rates than airside kiosks). Note that while Visa and Mastercard contactless taps work directly at Airport Rail Link gates, connecting BTS Skytrain or MRT lines may use separate tickets, cards or tokens — factor that in if travelling with large suitcases at rush hour.

Tips for Level 1 official public taxis

Ignore unsolicited taxi offers inside the Level 2 Arrivals hall and ride the escalator down to Level 1, Gates 4–7. Press the kiosk button to print your lane ticket (keep the slip — it contains driver details). Confirm the driver switches the meter on; the 50 THB airport surcharge is paid at drop-off along with expressway toll fees (typically 75 THB total via Sirat/motorway routes).

4. Quick BKK arrival checklist

  1. Watch for S-gates (SAT-1) on arrival. Budget an extra 10–15 minutes to ride the underground APM shuttle back to the Main Terminal before passport control.
  2. Save your hotel name & address in both English and Thai. Screenshot your hotel map pin offline — very helpful for Level 1 public taxi drivers.
  3. Carry small Thai baht notes if taking a public taxi. Keep 20, 50 and 100 THB notes for motorway toll booths and the 50 THB airport surcharge.
  4. Install your Thailand eSIM before departure. Have mobile data working as soon as you clear customs so Grab, Google Maps and hotel chats work straight away.

Common questions

Do I clear immigration inside the SAT-1 satellite terminal at BKK?

No — passengers arriving at SAT-1 (S-gates) ride the free underground Automated People Mover (APM) train to the Main Terminal first, where all immigration, baggage belts and transport exits are located.

What time does the Bangkok Airport Rail Link stop running?

The Airport Rail Link operates roughly from 05:30 until around midnight daily. If your flight lands late in the evening — factoring in immigration and baggage reclaim — plan a Level 1 metered taxi, Grab or pre-booked private transfer instead.

How much is the extra fee at the BKK public taxi stand?

Every metered taxi departing from the Level 1 public taxi kiosks carries a mandatory 50 THB airport surcharge, plus expressway tolls paid by the passenger along the route into Bangkok.

Can I reach Don Mueang Airport (DMK) on the Airport Rail Link?

No. The Airport Rail Link serves BKK only. If transferring between BKK and DMK, look for the free AOT inter-airport shuttle bus (with valid same-day onward DMK flight confirmation) or allow 60–90+ minutes by road depending on traffic.
