1. Know your CUN arrival terminal

Terminals 2, 3 and 4 sit in separate buildings connected only by free airport shuttle vans — you cannot walk comfortably between terminals with luggage in Quintana Roo heat.

Terminal 3

Major UK, US & Canadian Airlines

Serves British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, American, Delta and many North American/European long-haul flights. ADO ticket booth and pickup bays sit directly outside.

Terminal 4

International & Full-Service Hub

Modern terminal serving Aeromexico, Air France, Southwest, JetBlue, TUI/charters and partner airlines, with clear outdoor ground-transport lanes and ADO counter.

Terminal 2 · Shuttles

Regional, Charter & Tren Maya Hub

Handles Viva, Volaris, budget carriers and select charters. Main connecting hub for inter-terminal vans and the feeder shuttle out to the Cancún Airport Tren Maya station.

2. Getting from Cancún Airport (CUN) to your resort

Leaving CUN is famously chaotic if you walk outside without a plan: Uber is prohibited from picking up at airport curbs, and walk-up terminal taxi prices are high compared to pre-booked transfers or ADO buses.

Tips for ADO buses at CUN

Step outside the terminal doors (past the indoor booths) and look for the red-and-purple ADO booth curbside. Buses have air conditioning and stow suitcases underneath. Keep small Mexican pesos or a Visa/Mastercard handy — and if staying deep in Costa Mujeres or an all-inclusive south of Playa del Carmen, remember ADO only stops at town terminals, meaning a taxi leg is still needed at the far end.

Tips for authorized pickups

Before walking through the customs exit hallway, read your transfer voucher’s exact outside meeting point (for example “Friends & Family bar” or a numbered bay at T3/T4). Keep walking past anyone inside wearing an unofficial lanyard who asks “Who is your driver?”

3. Mexico mobile data & eSIM on arrival

CUN terminal Wi-Fi slows down heavily when multiple widebody jets land together — right when you need to pull up your transfer voucher, confirm an outside driver’s WhatsApp message or navigate in Spanish. Standard UK and European mobile plans charge steep daily roaming fees in Mexico.

Check whether your home plan covers Mexico without daily caps. If not, installing a Mexico travel eSIM on home Wi-Fi before departure gets you connected on Mexican LTE/5G networks the moment your aircraft taxis to the gate.

4. Quick Cancún arrival checklist

  1. Confirm your terminal (T2, T3 or T4) & immigration line time. Passport control and baggage reclaim regularly take 30–60+ minutes at peak US/UK arrival banks.
  2. Save your pickup instructions & hotel address offline. Screenshot your voucher meeting point before clearing Customs so you never stop inside the timeshare corridor.
  3. Don’t rely on calling an Uber at the airport curb. Pre-book an authorized private transfer or catch the official ADO bus outside the doors.
  4. Install your Mexico eSIM before flying. Keep WhatsApp and maps working immediately when stepping outside into the pickup bays.

Common questions

Can I get an Uber from Cancún Airport to the Hotel Zone?

No — federal airport rules ban Uber and other rideshare pickups on Cancún Airport grounds. You can take an Uber to CUN for departure, or take an ADO bus into Downtown Cancún and order an Uber from there, otherwise use a pre-booked transfer or official airport cab.

Should I buy an ADO bus ticket online before my flight to Cancún?

Usually no — long-haul flight delays plus passport and customs queues vary wildly. Since ADO desks sit right outside Terminals 2, 3 and 4 with frequent departures, buying the next available seat after collecting luggage is safer.

Who are all the people offering “free info” right after Customs at CUN?

Almost all counters inside the customs exit hallway sell resort timeshare presentations or marked-up shuttle tickets. Politely walk straight through the outdoor sliding doors before stopping for transport.

How long is the transfer from CUN to Playa del Carmen or Tulum?

Cancún’s Hotel Zone takes ~20–35 minutes, Playa del Carmen ~50–65 minutes, and Tulum ~90–120 minutes depending on Highway 307 roadworks and time of day.
