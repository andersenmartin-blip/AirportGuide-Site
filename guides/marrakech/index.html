1. Marrakech Menara (RAK) arrivals & Medina access

Terminals 1 and 2 form one combined passenger building with clear signage straight out to the forecourt — your main logistics occur at passport queues and finding your Riad door inside the old city.

Menara Arrivals

Terminal Concourse & Passport Control

Allow 30–60+ minutes for passport stamps during busy European arrival waves. Note that Ryanair and low-cost flights still require a printed paper boarding pass or stamped desk check on departures — keep copies handy.

Forecourt Exit

ALSA Bus 19 & Official Taxi Rank

Just outside the arrivals terminal doors you will find the posted official taxi rate board, taxi rank kiosk and the clearly signed ALSA Bus 19 airport express stop.

Medina Riads · Pedestrian Only

Nearest Gate (Bab) Drop-Off

Most Medina Riads sit down narrow pedestrian alleys. Taxis and private drivers leave you at the closest drivable gate (e.g., Jemaa el-Fna, Bab Doukkala, Bab Laksour) — expect a 5–15 minute walk or pre-arranged Riad escort.

2. Getting from RAK to the Medina or Gueliz

Distance is short (~6–8 km), so travel choice comes down to cash readiness, late-night landing surcharges and whether you want someone coordinating directly with your Riad host.

Tips for ALSA Bus 19 & cash

Exchange a small amount or withdraw Moroccan dirhams (MAD) at an airport ATM in the arrivals concourse before boarding Bus 19, as the driver collects the 30 MAD fare in cash (small bills preferred). Remember that Moroccan dirhams are a closed currency — plan ATMs accordingly and check your bank’s foreign ATM fee.

Tips for navigating to your Riad

Before boarding your flight, ask your Riad host for: (1) their WhatsApp number, (2) the name of the nearest car-accessible Medina gate or square, and (3) whether they can send a staff member to meet your taxi at drop-off. Avoid accepting unsolicited street offers to “show you the closed road” once inside the Medina.

3. Morocco mobile data & eSIM on arrival

Walking through Marrakech’s Medina alleys for the first time without offline/live Google Maps or WhatsApp access to your Riad is stressful — and Morocco sits outside EU/UK roaming zones with some of the highest out-of-bundle roaming charges if left unchecked.

Check your home mobile plan’s Morocco rate before takeoff. Buying and installing a travel eSIM at home (alongside saving an offline Maps area for Marrakech) ensures you can message your Riad and check walking pins the minute you step outside Menara.

4. Quick Marrakech arrival checklist

  1. Confirm your Riad’s nearest accessible gate (Bab) & WhatsApp contact. Save the exact GPS pin offline before landing.
  2. Have small Moroccan dirhams (MAD) for Bus 19 or curb taxis. Bus 19 costs 30 MAD cash; official night taxis rise after 22:00.
  3. Sort out Morocco eSIM data & offline maps before takeoff. Avoid heavy non-EU roaming fees while navigating the Medina.
  4. Don’t forget paper boarding pass rules on return! Many low-cost carriers departing RAK still require a printed boarding pass or desk check at the airport.

Common questions

Can a taxi or private transfer drive straight up to my Riad in the Medina?

Only if your Riad sits directly on one of the few paved edge roads. Most Riads sit inside pedestrian-only alleys, so vehicles stop at the nearest gate (Bab) or square — coordinate a quick walk or Riad porter in advance.

Is there a train from Marrakech Airport (RAK) to the city?

No rail line reaches Marrakech Airport directly. ALSA Bus 19 connects the airport to Marrakech’s main ONCF railway station (Gare ONCF) in Gueliz as well as Jemaa el-Fna.

How much should an official airport taxi to the Medina cost?

Regulated daytime airport rank fares generally run around 100–150 MAD (roughly €9–€14), rising to around 150–240 MAD at night after 22:00. Always verify the posted board outside the terminal and confirm the total before riding.

Do I need Moroccan dirhams straight away at RAK?

Yes if taking ALSA Bus 19 (30 MAD cash), buying a local SIM or paying a street taxi. If pre-booking a private airport transfer online, your ride is already settled before you land.
