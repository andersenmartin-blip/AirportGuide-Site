1. Know your Antalya (AYT) terminal

Antalya’s two international terminals sit roughly 1.5–2 km apart along separate road spurs, which directly affects tram access and meetup spots.

Terminal 1 & Domestic

Connected Main Zone

Interconnected terminal complex sitting directly next to the elevated AntRay (T1A) tram station, taxi rank and bus stops.

Terminal 2 · No Tram Stop

Separate International Terminal

Many European leisure/charter airlines use Terminal 2. The AntRay tram does not stop at T2 — take the free airport ring shuttle bus (~15–20 min frequency) to T1 if using the tram, or walk out to Bus 600 / taxis.

Coastal Distances

Lara · Belek · Side · Alanya

AYT sits east of Antalya city: Lara/Kundu is 15–25 min east, Belek 40–50 min, Side ~1h 15m and Alanya ~2 hours along the D400 highway.

2. Getting from AYT to Antalya city or coastal resorts

AntRay and city buses serve Antalya’s city center and Old Town (Kaleiçi) well, whereas coastal resort belts (Belek, Side, Alanya) lie east away from the tram network.

Tips for AntRay & Antalya city stays

For Old Town (Kaleiçi) boutique hotels, take the T1A tram from Terminal 1 toward Fatih and get off around İsmetpaşa or Muratpaşa (within ~10 minutes’ walk of Kaleiçi lanes). Confirm contactless bank-card tap readiness or grab an AntalyaKart at the kiosk.

Tips for Belek, Side & Alanya resorts

Belek golf resorts, Kundu palaces and Side hotel zones sit well off the D400 highway — always verify your hotel’s exact transfer zone and distance before opting for public intercity coaches.

Common questions

Does the AntRay tram stop at Antalya Terminal 2?

No — the AntRay T1A airport tram station serves Terminal 1 and the Domestic Terminal only. If you land at Terminal 2, take the free airport ring shuttle to Terminal 1 to board the tram, or catch a taxi, transfer or Bus 600 directly from T2.

Can I reach Belek or Alanya by tram from Antalya Airport?

No. The tram runs west into Antalya city center (and Expo), while Belek, Side and Alanya lie east along the Mediterranean coast. Public transit to Alanya involves riding Bus 600/tram to Antalya Otogar and switching onto a ~2-hour intercity coach.

Are taxis metered cleanly from AYT?

Official airport rank taxis run on meters (or posted destination estimates at the curb). For nearby Lara/Kundu (~15–25 min) or Kaleiçi, taxis are quick; for Side or Alanya, a pre-booked fixed transfer avoids a climbing long-distance meter.

Is airport Wi-Fi free at Antalya (AYT)?

Terminal Wi-Fi exists in limited free windows via SMS/kiosk verification, which often stalls if your home SIM cannot receive roaming SMS reliably — having a Turkey eSIM ready avoids relying on kiosk Wi-Fi codes.
