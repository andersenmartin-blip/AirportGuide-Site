1. Know your DXB arrival terminal

DXB’s three terminals sit on opposite sides of the airfield. Confirm your arrival terminal before planning your onward ride into Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah or Deira.

Terminal 3

Emirates & Qantas

Direct Red Line Metro access, 24/7 official taxi rank and Arrivals meet-and-greet area. Concourses A, B and C are large — allow 25–40 minutes from touch-down to baggage reclaim.

Terminal 1

International Airlines

Used by most non-Emirates international carriers (Concourse D connects by airport train). Direct Red Line Metro station, 24/7 taxi rank and pre-booked pickup hall.

Terminal 2 · No Metro

flydubai & Regional

Located on the north side of the airport with no direct Metro station. Plan an official taxi, a pre-booked private transfer, or an RTA bus (nol card required).

2. Getting from DXB to your hotel

Match your transport choice to your arrival terminal, landing time and luggage. Public transport is inexpensive along the Red Line corridor, while door-to-door taxis and pre-booked transfers save changing with heavy bags or after midnight.

Tips for the Dubai Metro

Follow the indoor signs to the Red Line at Terminal 1 or Terminal 3 and purchase a Silver or Gold nol card (or single Red ticket) at the ticket counter or vending machines. Remember that your scheduled landing time is not the time you reach the platform — factor in immigration and baggage reclaim, and verify current DXB Metro operating hours if you land in the late evening or early Sunday morning.

Tips for official taxis

Ignore unofficial touts inside the terminal and join the signed RTA Airport Taxi queue outside Arrivals (cream-coloured taxis; pink-roofed taxis driven by women are also available for women and families). Confirm current airport flag-fall details on Dubai Airports’ official taxi page.

3. UAE mobile data & eSIM on arrival

Free DXB airport Wi-Fi works inside the terminal while you wait at baggage claim. Once you step outside to meet a driver, check Google Maps, or navigate from a Metro stop to your hotel, having mobile data active immediately avoids stress — and home-carrier roaming in the UAE is frequently excluded from standard European/North American bundles.

Check your existing mobile plan’s UAE roaming rate first. If UAE data isn’t included, installing a travel eSIM on an unlocked, compatible phone before departure lets you connect as soon as the plane lands, without queuing at arrival SIM kiosks.

4. Quick DXB arrival checklist

  1. Verify DXB & your terminal (T1, T2 or T3). Check live gate and baggage belt details so you know which exit you’re using.
  2. Save your hotel address offline. Keep a screenshot of the hotel name, area (e.g. Downtown, Marina, Palm, Deira) and booking confirmation.
  3. Set up mobile data before takeoff. Confirm UAE roaming or install your eSIM while you still have reliable home Wi-Fi.
  4. Match your ride to your landing hour. If arriving near midnight, on Sunday morning, or into Terminal 2, plan a 24/7 taxi or pre-booked transfer instead of relying on the Metro.

Common questions

Can I take the Dubai Metro from every DXB terminal?

Red Line stations are directly inside Terminals 1 and 3. Terminal 2 does not have a Metro station — plan an official taxi, pre-booked transfer, or RTA bus with a nol card.

What if my flight lands at DXB late at night?

The Metro closes overnight (hours vary by day of week, with a later start on Sundays), and passport control plus baggage collection take extra time. Official RTA airport taxis and pre-booked private transfers operate 24/7.

Do I need a UAE eSIM if DXB has free Wi-Fi?

DXB terminal Wi-Fi works indoors, which helps at Arrivals. Once you leave the terminal for your hotel, Metro walk or ride-pickup, you will only have data if your home plan covers UAE roaming or you use a local SIM / travel eSIM.
