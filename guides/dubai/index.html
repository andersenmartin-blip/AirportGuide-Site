Taking the metro

Follow signs to the Red Line at Terminal 1 or 3. Buy a ticket or nol card at the station. Check the station nearest your hotel and any onward connection before travelling. For Terminal 2, plan a bus or taxi connection instead of expecting a terminal metro station.

Service hours vary by day, with a later start on Sundays. Your landing time is not the time you will reach the platform: allow for passport control and baggage collection. Check the airport’s current metro information before relying on a late-night connection.

Taking a taxi or private transfer

Official airport taxis operate around the clock from designated terminal ranks. Follow the taxi signs after Arrivals. The airport publishes a starting fare, with additional journey charges; confirm the current fare information on its taxi page.

If you book a transfer, save the meeting instructions and check what happens if your flight is delayed. Compare the quote with your group size and bags in mind.

Will your phone work when you land?

Check whether your existing plan includes UAE roaming and how much data it allows. If it already covers the trip, you may not need another plan. Otherwise, compare a roaming add-on, local SIM or travel eSIM.

For an eSIM, check that your phone supports it and is carrier-unlocked. Review the package’s coverage, data allowance, validity and activation rules. Some packages provide data without a local phone number. Save the setup instructions while you have a reliable connection.

A simple arrival checklist

  1. Confirm DXB and your terminal. Keep your flight number available and check the airport displays for the latest baggage information.
  2. Save your hotel address. A screenshot helps if your connection is unavailable.
  3. Choose a route after collecting your bags. Recheck metro availability or your booked pickup instructions if your arrival changes.
  4. Keep the first journey simple. Know your final stop, meeting point or hotel address before leaving the terminal.

Common questions

Can I take the metro from every DXB terminal?

The airport lists Red Line stations at Terminals 1 and 3. If you arrive at Terminal 2, plan a connecting bus or taxi journey and check the route first.

What if my flight arrives late at night?

Check current metro operating hours and allow time to leave Arrivals. Official airport taxis operate 24/7. For a booked transfer, use the provider’s meeting and delay instructions.

Do I need to buy an eSIM?

Check your existing UAE roaming allowance first. An eSIM is one option if you need extra data and have a compatible, unlocked phone.
