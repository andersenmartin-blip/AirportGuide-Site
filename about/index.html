About Airport Guide

Airport Guide is an independent web app for travellers who want instant flight details and practical airport arrival information — without creating an account or handing over personal travel documents.

Updated November 2026

Live flight status in one clean card

Enter a flight number to view departure and arrival times in each airport’s local time zone, terminal and gate assignments, baggage reclaim belts, destination weather and live delay status. Our lookup service combines live aviation feeds (AeroDataBox and Airlabs) so a missing field in one feed can be supplemented by the other.

Flight schedules and gates can change rapidly at the airport. Always verify final boarding gates and baggage belts on airport terminal displays and with your airline.

100% local boarding-pass reading

When you select a boarding-pass photo or PDF on the front page, barcode and text extraction runs 100% locally inside your browser on your own device. Your boarding pass file, passenger name and booking reference are never uploaded to our servers and are never sent to Google Analytics. Only the extracted flight number (for example, EK32) is used to fetch live public flight status.

Practical, independent arrival guides

Our airport arrival guides — starting with Dubai International Airport (DXB) — are written to help travellers compare public transport (such as metro and public buses) openly alongside taxis, private transfers and travel eSIM options, backed by links to official airport documentation.

How Airport Guide is funded

Airport Guide is free to use without sign-up walls. Some travel links on flight results and arrival guides are affiliate partner links (including Airalo for travel eSIMs, Kiwitaxi for airport transfers, and AirHelp for flight disruption claims). If you choose to buy or book through a labelled partner link, Airport Guide may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Public transport options and official airport resources are always listed independently.

For full details on optional analytics consent and partner attribution, read our Privacy & cookies page.
