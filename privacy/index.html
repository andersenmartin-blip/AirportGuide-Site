Privacy & cookies

Updated 6 November 2026

Your boarding pass

Airport Guide reads uploaded boarding passes in your browser. The file is not uploaded to our flight service or sent to Google Analytics. If a flight number can be read, we use that number to look up the flight. Remove the file from the result to clear its preview, or close the page.

Flight searches and saved searches

A flight lookup sends the flight number to our Cloudflare service, which requests data from Airlabs and AeroDataBox. Your browser also downloads airport data and may request destination weather. These services receive the information needed to answer the request. Flight details can be included in a URL when you choose to share a flight.

Recent flight searches are kept in this browser so you can reopen them. They are not an account or a passenger history stored on our server. Hosting, API providers and external asset providers may process normal request information such as an IP address under their own policies.

Optional visit measurement

If you allow analytics, we load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-YYW1KW4CGN) to understand visits, successful flight searches, Dubai-guide views and clicks on travel links. Analytics cookies can distinguish visits in the same browser. Our custom measurement does not include boarding-pass files, names, booking references or flight numbers.

The Google tag is blocked until you allow analytics. Advertising storage and advertising personalization remain disabled. You can use the flight search and travel links without allowing analytics.

We remember your analytics choice in this browser for up to 180 days. at any time. Withdrawing consent clears Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded tag.

Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites for details of its processing.

Travel partner links

Some links use affiliate tracking from Airalo/Impact, Kiwitaxi/Travelpayouts or AirHelp. A purchase or booking may earn Airport Guide a commission at no extra cost to you. We label affiliate links separately from ordinary links.

Opening a partner link takes you to an external service. The link can identify Airport Guide, the page placement, destination airport and our named campaign, so that the partner can attribute clicks and bookings. It does not contain your boarding pass, passenger details or a unique Airport Guide visitor ID. The partner or tracking network may set its own cookies and offers its own privacy and consent choices.

Clicks are not purchases or confirmed commission. Partner terms, availability, prices, privacy policies and cancellation conditions apply on the external site.
